Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers for better oral care

Can medication completely relieve tooth pain?

Not entirely. While medications may offer short-term relief, long-lasting comfort requires identifying and treating the root cause.

How often should I see a dentist?

Every six months for routine check-ups, cleaning, and preventive care.

Do all wisdom teeth need to be removed?

No. Removal is recommended only if they are impacted, infected, or causing pain.

What is the best age to get braces?

Between 12 and 14 years is common, but treatment can be beneficial at other ages too.
