Treatments

Our clinic offers a wide range of dental treatments delivered with precision, safety, and patient comfort in mind.

Teeth Cleaning & Scaling

Advanced, non-surgical gum care using modern LASER technology for precise and effective treatment.

Tooth Filling

Restore decayed or damaged teeth with durable materials that preserve strength and appearance.

Tooth Extraction

Gentle extraction services performed with a focus on comfort, safety, and smooth recovery.

Artificial Complete Denture

Precision-crafted dentures that restore both function and confidence for patients with missing teeth.

Dental Implants

Biocompatible implant solutions that create stable, natural-feeling replacements for missing teeth.

Laser Dentistry

Minimally invasive procedures for faster healing, reduced discomfort, and enhanced precision.

Root Canal Therapy

Carefully relieve infection, eliminate pain, and preserve the natural tooth structure whenever possible.

Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Gentle and effective removal of impacted wisdom teeth to protect long-term oral health.

Fixed Partial Denture (Bridge)

Custom bridges that restore missing teeth and improve chewing, comfort, and smile aesthetics.

Teeth Whitening (Bleaching)

Professional whitening treatment to brighten stains and restore a radiant smile.

Veneers

Custom-made shells that correct chips, discoloration, and small gaps for a refined smile.

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle care designed to help children feel comfortable while building lifelong healthy habits.

Flap Surgery

Support advanced gum health with careful procedures to remove deep bacterial buildup.

Orthodontic Braces

Customized realignment plans to improve bite function and create a confident smile.

Clear Aligners

Discreet, removable aligners for a comfortable, flexible orthodontic alternative.
